From an idea to a measured result.

Fabrik helps its owner collect useful evidence, prepare videos, review the finished work, publish to authorized destinations, and understand what happened.

This is an owner-operated experimental workspace, run by Will Clark. It is not open for public account registration. These pages explain the app; they do not grant access to its private workspace.

What the workspace does

  • Keeps ideas, content, review evidence, publication links, notes and measured outcomes together.
  • Separates a prepared file from an actual publication, and a measured zero from unavailable data.
  • Uses dated results to guide the next content decision. Views and subscribers are distinct outcomes.

Why it connects to YouTube

The integration, shown in Google consent as Fabrik YouTube, uses YouTube API Services. With the owner's permission it can verify the selected channel, upload approved videos, read channel/video information, and collect available YouTube Analytics reports.

Each connection is bound to an exact channel. Connecting an account does not itself schedule or authorize a new upload. Publication also needs the approved content, destination, rights and quality checks, and enabled publishing controls.

The cloud worker renews short-lived access tokens using authorized offline credentials. Google or the account owner can revoke access; permanent access is not guaranteed.
