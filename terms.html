Accounts and publication

Only connect an account you control and have authority to use. Authorization is specific to the selected account and permissions. A connection is not permission to publish any arbitrary content or expand access to another account.

Before publication, the content must have the required rights, review and destination approval. Do not use Fabrik for impersonation, spam, artificial views or engagement, or attempts to bypass a platform's access controls. An ambiguous upload must be reconciled before another upload is attempted.

Account grants can expire or be revoked. Connecting YouTube does not remove its own limits or policies. The current experimental setup can require operator intervention.
